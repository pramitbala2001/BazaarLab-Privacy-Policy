Privacy Policy for BazaarLab

App Name: BazaarLab (com.bazaarlab)
Developer: NPL Studios
Effective Date: November 19, 2026
Contact Email: info.nplstudios@gmail.com

Educational Paper Trading Simulator: BazaarLab is a 100% virtual NSE & BSE Indian equity paper trading simulator. No real money deposits, withdrawals, bank/UPI links, or real-money brokerage services exist in this application. BazaarLab is not affiliated with NSE, BSE, or SEBI.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data needed to operate your virtual trading account, deliver optional notifications, handle customer support requests, and display ads:

Data Type Source Purpose
Name, Email Address & Account Identifier Google Sign-In or Email & Password Account authentication, profile identification, virtual capital preferences, and support ticket ownership.
Support Messages & Inbox Notices User-submitted support queries Responding to customer support queries and delivering in-app announcements.
Notification Token Device notification service Sending optional market announcements, price alert updates, and support replies (only if notification permission is granted).
Advertising ID & Diagnostics Third-party advertising partners Serving banner and optional rewarded video ads, fraud prevention, and ad performance measurement.
On-Device Paper Trading Data Stored locally on your device Saving your simulated holdings, intraday positions, orders, watchlists, trade logs, and price alerts locally on your device.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect bank account numbers, UPI IDs, PAN/Aadhaar numbers, debit/credit card numbers, or real brokerage credentials.
  • We do not collect precise GPS location, contacts, photos, microphone audio, or SMS messages.
  • We do not sell your personal information to data brokers.

3. Third-Party Services

BazaarLab uses trusted third-party service providers that process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Account & Hosting Services: Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Services: Google Advertising Privacy & Terms
  • Public Market Quote Feeds: Stock symbols and delayed/indicative NSE & BSE price quotes are fetched securely without attaching your personal identity.

4. Data Security & Encryption

All network communication between BazaarLab and external services is encrypted in transit using industry-standard encryption. Account records, inbox notices, and support conversations are protected by strict access controls restricted to the authenticated account owner and authorized support personnel.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your account records only while your account remains active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time directly inside the app:

  1. Open BazaarLab and go to the Funds tab.
  2. Scroll to the Legal, Privacy & Disclosure card.
  3. Tap Delete Account & Data and confirm Delete Forever.

Full deletion instructions and external deletion request options are available on our dedicated Account Deletion page.

6. Children's Privacy

BazaarLab is intended for general audiences interested in learning about stock market mechanics and is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us at info.nplstudios@gmail.com so we can delete it promptly.
